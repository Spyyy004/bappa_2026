Mumbai Ganpati 2026 Pandal Guide

Mumbai's Ganesh Chaturthi is one of the city's biggest annual celebrations, with famous Ganpati pandals spread across neighbourhoods including Lalbaug, Parel, Girgaon, Khetwadi, Matunga, Sion, Fort, Andheri and Chembur. PandalHop helps you turn that huge list into a practical darshan plan.

Plan Ganpati darshan around your time

Choose your date, starting point, time budget and preferred way of travelling. PandalHop then ranks worthwhile stops using estimated queue time, travel time and the importance of each pandal, rather than simply giving you the shortest route.

Popular Ganpati pandals in Mumbai

The planner includes major 2026 stops such as Lalbaugcha Raja, Mumbaicha Raja at Ganesh Galli, Chinchpoklicha Chintamani, Tejukaya Cha Raja, GSB Seva Mandal Ganpati, Khetwadi Cha Ganraj, Girgaon Cha Raja and Andhericha Raja, alongside other neighbourhood pandals.

Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 in Mumbai

Ganesh Chaturthi begins on 14 September 2026, with the main Anant Chaturdashi visarjan on 25 September 2026. Queue lengths, entrances and special arrangements can change during the festival, so the numbers shown here are planning estimates rather than live guarantees.
